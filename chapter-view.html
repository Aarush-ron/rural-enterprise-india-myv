Chapter 2: Understanding EMIs

Language:

Concept Theory

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. Equated monthly installments are applied to both interest and principal each month so that over a specified number of years, the loan is paid off in full.

For a growing rural enterprise, utilizing EMIs can be a highly effective strategy for capital expenditure. Instead of depleting operational cash reserves to purchase heavy machinery, tractors, or cold storage units, the cost is distributed across the asset's usable lifespan, maintaining liquidity for day-to-day operations and emergencies.

Practical Scenario Check

Your shop's gross margin is ₹50,000 per month. You urgently need a commercial refrigerator costing ₹60,000 to store perishables. Which financial approach effectively protects your daily operational liquidity?

Assessment Passed

Selecting the EMI preserves your ₹50,000 cash flow, allowing you to seamlessly restock inventory, pay vendors, and handle daily expenses while simultaneously expanding your business capacity.
